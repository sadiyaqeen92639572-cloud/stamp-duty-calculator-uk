Buy-to-Let Stamp Duty — Same Rules as Second Homes

HMRC, Revenue Scotland and the Welsh Revenue Authority all treat buy-to-let purchases identically to second homes for stamp duty purposes — the surcharge applies whenever you already own another residential property, anywhere in the world.

Multiple Dwellings Relief (MDR) can reduce the bill on qualifying multi-unit purchases (e.g. buying a property with an annexe, or several units in one transaction) — see the refund calculator if you think this was missed on a past purchase.
