Stamp Duty Payment Deadlines

Miss the deadline and HMRC, Revenue Scotland or the Welsh Revenue Authority can charge interest and penalties — here’s exactly how long you have.

Deadlines by Nation

NationTaxDeadline from completion
England / NISDLT14 days
ScotlandLBTT30 days
WalesLTT30 days

In practice, your solicitor or conveyancer usually files the return and pays the tax on your behalf out of funds held for completion — but the legal responsibility to file and pay on time is yours as the buyer.

What Happens If You're Late

Missing the deadline can trigger both a fixed penalty and daily interest on the unpaid amount, even if the return itself was filed on time but payment lagged. If you're using a solicitor, confirm with them in writing that the return has been filed and payment made — don't assume.
