MDR can reduce stamp duty when a single transaction includes more than one dwelling — for example a house with a self-contained annexe, or several flats bought together. It is one of the most commonly missed reliefs; if your purchase included more than one dwelling and your solicitor did not mention MDR, it is worth checking.
For the England/NI second-home surcharge refund (after selling your previous home within 36 months), you can apply directly via gov.uk or through a specialist reclaim firm on a no-win-no-fee basis. Reclaim firms typically handle the HMRC paperwork for a percentage of the amount recovered.
